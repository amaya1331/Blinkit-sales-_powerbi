#  BlinkIT Grocery Data Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **BlinkIT Grocery Data Analysis Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze grocery sales performance, outlet characteristics, product categories, and customer ratings.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence workflow**, including data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and interactive dashboard design.

---

##  Project Overview

BlinkIT is a quick-commerce grocery platform where understanding product performance, outlet characteristics, customer ratings, and sales distribution is important for making data-driven business decisions.

The objective of this project is to analyze the BlinkIT grocery dataset and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides insights into:

* Overall sales performance
* Product category performance
* Fat-content-wise sales
* Outlet performance
* Outlet size and location analysis
* Outlet type comparison
* Product visibility
* Customer ratings
* Outlet establishment trends

The dashboard allows users to interact with different filters and quickly identify important sales and operational trends.

---

##  Business Objectives

The project aims to answer questions such as:

1. What is the overall sales performance?
2. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
3. How does sales performance vary by item fat content?
4. Which outlet types contribute the most to total sales?
5. Which outlet locations generate the highest revenue?
6. How does outlet size affect sales?
7. How do product visibility and sales relate to each other?
8. What is the average customer rating?
9. How has outlet performance changed based on establishment year?
10. Which product and outlet segments should receive greater business attention?

##  Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was prepared before creating the dashboard.

Key preparation activities included:

* Inspecting dataset structure
* Checking data types
* Handling missing values
* Standardizing categorical values
* Reviewing inconsistent values in `Item Fat Content`
* Checking numerical columns
* Validating sales and rating values
* Preparing fields for Power BI analysis

One important data-quality issue was the presence of different representations of fat-content values such as:

* `Low Fat`
* `low fat`
* `LF`
* `Regular`
* `reg`

These values can be standardized during Power Query transformation to ensure accurate aggregation and visualization.

##  Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Overall Sales Performance

The dashboard provides a high-level view of total sales and average sales, allowing users to quickly evaluate overall grocery business performance.

---

### 2. Sales by Item Type

The analysis identifies the product categories contributing most to sales.

The leading categories in the dataset include:

* **Fruits and Vegetables**
* **Snack Foods**
* **Household**
* **Frozen Foods**
* **Dairy**

Fruits and Vegetables generate the highest sales among the product categories in the dataset.

---

### 3. Sales by Item Fat Content

Sales are analyzed across different fat-content categories.

After considering the different labels used in the raw dataset, **Low Fat products contribute the largest share of sales**.

This analysis can help identify consumer preferences and product portfolio opportunities.

---

### 4. Sales by Outlet Location

The dashboard compares sales across:

* Tier 1
* Tier 2
* Tier 3

The dataset shows that **Tier 3 locations generate the highest total sales**, followed by Tier 2 and Tier 1.

This indicates that location tier is an important dimension for understanding BlinkIT's sales distribution.

---

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size

Outlet performance is compared across:

* Small
* Medium
* High

**Medium-sized outlets generate the highest total sales** in the dataset.

This provides a useful indication of the relationship between outlet scale and sales performance.

---

### 6. Sales by Outlet Type

The dashboard compares different outlet formats, including:

* Supermarket Type 1
* Supermarket Type 2
* Supermarket Type 3
* Grocery Store

**Supermarket Type 1** contributes the largest share of total sales among the outlet types.

---

### 7. Product Visibility

`Item Visibility` is analyzed to understand how product exposure relates to sales.

This can help identify products with:

* High visibility and high sales
* High visibility and low sales
* Low visibility and high sales
* Low visibility and low sales

These segments can provide opportunities for inventory, placement, and promotional decisions.

---

### 8. Customer Ratings

The dataset has an average rating of approximately **3.97/5**.

The dashboard enables ratings to be analyzed across different products and outlets, helping identify areas where customer satisfaction can potentially be improved.

---

### 9. Outlet Establishment Analysis

Outlet establishment year is used to analyze the distribution and performance of outlets across different years.

This helps understand how outlet age and establishment patterns relate to sales performance.

---

##  Key Insights

Based on the analysis of the provided dataset:

*  Total sales are approximately **$1.20 million**.
*  The average customer rating is approximately **3.97**.
*  **Fruits and Vegetables** are the highest-selling product category.
*  **Snack Foods** are the second-highest-selling category.
*  **Household** products also contribute significantly to overall sales.
*  **Tier 3 outlets** generate the highest sales among location tiers.
*  **Medium-sized outlets** generate the highest sales among outlet sizes.
*  **Supermarket Type 1** is the strongest outlet type by total sales.
*  **Low Fat** products account for the largest sales contribution after standardizing fat-content labels.

##  DAX & Calculations

The project can use DAX measures for important metrics such as:

```DAX
Total Sales = SUM('BlinkIT Grocery Data'[Total Sales])
```

```DAX
Average Sales = AVERAGE('BlinkIT Grocery Data'[Total Sales])
```

```DAX
Average Rating = AVERAGE('BlinkIT Grocery Data'[Rating])
```

```DAX
Total Items = COUNTROWS('BlinkIT Grocery Data')
```

Additional measures can be created for category contribution, outlet performance, percentage of total sales, and other business KPIs.
